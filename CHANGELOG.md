# Coinspire changelog

Greg and Sarah's shared money app. Versions follow MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH.

## 4.7.0 · 2026-09-30 · A real payoff plan
- The Debt tab now leads with a Payoff Plan: pick avalanche (highest rate first), snowball (smallest balance first) or a hybrid, add an extra amount, and see the month you are debt free.
- Rollover works: when a debt is paid off, its whole monthly payment moves to the next one. On our tested numbers that is 39 months instead of 73, with about $1,500 less interest.
- Month by month shows each step, what is left on every debt, and which order we'd pick.
- On a phone the plan and its controls share one screen, the extra amounts are chips, and every button is big enough to tap.
- Fixed: the dashboard no longer breaks when income is $0, and the header month picker looks right in light mode.

## 4.6.0 · 2026-05-01 · Sign-in with a PIN
- A PIN signs you in, checked on the server, and the app reads your own data with your own session.

## 4.5.0 · 2026-05-01 · Onboarding
- A new person starts with their own blank setup, and Greg's numbers never flash on screen for anyone else.

## 4.4.0 · 2026-05-01 · Splits that add up
- Totals follow the split between you, and an error in one part of the app no longer takes down the rest.

## 4.0.0 · 2026-03 · The app, rebuilt
- Bills, splits, budget, debt, savings, goals, credit, net worth and a calendar, with a dashboard you can arrange, onboarding for a second person, and your data kept safe across reloads.
